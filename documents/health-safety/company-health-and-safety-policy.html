Health & safety

Company Health & Safety Policy

A short, signed statement of how Portobello Plumbing Co looks after people. The day-to-day system sits in the Health & Safety Management Plan and the Harm Register.

1. Our commitment

Portobello Plumbing Company Limited, trading as Portobello Plumbing Co, is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for workers, contractors, visitors and anyone else who could be affected by our work, including customers, occupants and members of the public. We treat health and safety as part of doing the job properly, not as extra paperwork after the fact.

We will so far as is reasonably practicable:

  • prevent work-related injury, illness and harm;
  • identify hazards, assess risks and put effective controls in place;
  • provide the information, training, plant and personal protective equipment people need to work safely;
  • consult workers and other PCBUs who share a workplace with us;
  • report, investigate and learn from incidents, near misses and notifiable events; and
  • review this policy and our wider system at least once a year, or sooner after a significant change or incident.

2. Who this applies to

This policy applies to all work carried out by or on behalf of Portobello Plumbing Co, including:

  • work at customer homes and other occupied sites across Auckland;
  • workshop, storage, yard and office activities at 15A Sainsbury Rd, Mt Albert;
  • travel between jobs; and
  • anyone working under our direction: the Director, occasional subcontract tradespeople, and any future apprentice or employee.

3. Legal framework

We will comply with the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, the Health and Safety at Work (General Risk and Workplace Management) Regulations 2016, and other applicable regulations, codes of practice and WorkSafe New Zealand guidance. That includes duties that apply to plumbing, ladder roof access, small service excavations, hot work, solvents, and work in occupied and tenanted homes.

4. Responsibilities

PCBU: Portobello Plumbing Company Limited

As a person conducting a business or undertaking (trading as Portobello Plumbing Co), we have the primary duty of care: to ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, the health and safety of workers and others affected by our work.

Officer: Jonathan David Ian Elton

The Director must exercise due diligence: keep up to date with H&S knowledge, understand the risks of our work, make sure there are resources and processes, and verify that those processes are used.

Workers

Take reasonable care for themselves and others, follow this policy and our procedures (including induction and hot-work permits when they apply), use the controls and PPE provided, and speak up about hazards, incidents and near misses. This includes subcontractors and any apprentice.

Customers and other PCBUs

Where we share a workplace, we will consult, cooperate and coordinate. We expect safe access, known hazards to be disclosed, and the right to stop work if a site is unsafe.

5. How we work

In practice, this policy means:

  • Plan the job. Risks are identified before work starts, using the Harm Register and a site-specific check. High-risk work is not started until controls are in place.
  • Stop if it is not safe. Anyone can pause or stop work without penalty if they reasonably believe there is a serious risk.
  • Stay competent. Plumbing work is carried out by a New Zealand registered plumber, or under appropriate supervision. Extra training is required for specialised tasks. Roof work is ladder-only. A workplace first-aid course, and a New Zealand asbestos awareness refresh, are planned actions for the Director.
  • Look after the site. We keep work areas tidy, protect occupants, and leave the place safer than we found it where we can.
  • Tell the truth about incidents. Injuries, near misses and damage are reported promptly so we can fix the cause, not hide it.

6. Objectives

  • No notifiable injuries or illnesses arising from our work.
  • Every job has a documented site risk check before high-risk tasks begin.
  • Incidents and near misses are recorded and reviewed within 7 days.
  • This policy, the Management Plan and the Harm Register are reviewed at least annually.
